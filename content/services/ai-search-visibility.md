sectionOneHeading: "What AI search visibility is"
sectionOne: |
  AI search visibility, or answer engine optimisation, is the work of being recommended when someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews for the best option in your category — rather than being absent from an answer that names five competitors.

  It is related to SEO but not the same thing. Traditional search returns a list of pages and lets the user choose. An answer engine returns a single synthesised recommendation, usually naming a handful of options. Ranking tenth on Google means you are on page one; being tenth in an answer engine's consideration set usually means you are not mentioned at all.

  Two things drive whether you appear. Whether the model can resolve "your brand" to a coherent entity with consistent facts attached, and whether the sources it retrieves from — community discussions, listicles, directories, review platforms — mention you in the right context.

sectionTwoHeading: "How it works"
sectionTwo: |
  ### 1. Measure the baseline first

  We run a fixed set of buyer-realistic prompts across multiple models and record, for each: whether you were mentioned, whether you were cited as a source, your position among named competitors, and which competitors appeared instead. Without this, every subsequent decision is guesswork, and the competitor set alone is usually informative — it is often not the one you see in Google.

  ### 2. Make the brand resolvable

  Structured data that identifies the organisation, consistent facts across every place you appear, and explicit connections between your site and your other profiles. If your founding date, location or service list differs across sources, models treat the entity as uncertain and reach for a competitor they can describe confidently.

  ### 3. Make the site readable to machines

  Answer engines strip styling and read text. Direct answers placed immediately under clear headings, questions phrased the way people ask them, and definitions stated plainly get extracted; the same information spread across an animated scroll section does not.

  ### 4. Get into the sources that get retrieved

  Answer engines synthesise from what already exists — community threads, comparison articles, directories, review platforms. If your category's answers are assembled from listicles you do not appear in, on-site work alone will not fix it. This is where [Reddit](/services/reddit-marketing) and [Quora](/services/quora-marketing) work becomes directly relevant.

  ### 5. Re-measure, on the same prompts

  The same prompt set, run on a schedule, so change is attributable rather than anecdotal. Model behaviour shifts on its own, so a fixed baseline is the only way to distinguish your progress from the model's drift.

sectionThreeHeading: "Who it's for"
sectionThree: |
  **This is a fit if** your buyers research before purchasing and have started asking AI tools for recommendations — which now covers most considered B2B and B2C categories. It is most valuable when you are a credible option that answer engines currently omit.

  **This is not a fit if** you expect a fixed ranking you can buy or guarantee. There is no submission form, no paid placement, and no lever that forces a model to name you. Anyone selling guaranteed AI rankings is selling something that does not exist.

  It is also premature if your brand has no independent presence anywhere. Answer engines synthesise from third-party sources; if nothing exists to synthesise, the first work is earning that presence, not optimising for retrieval.

  ### An honest note on measurement

  Answer engines are non-deterministic — the same prompt can return different answers on different days. We report mention rates across repeated runs and multiple models rather than single-run screenshots, because a screenshot proves nothing. We use this method on our own visibility too.

  - question: "How is AEO different from SEO?"
    answer: "SEO optimises for a ranked list of links, where being on page one has value at any position. AEO optimises for inclusion in a single synthesised answer that names only a few options — so it is closer to all-or-nothing. AEO also depends much more heavily on what third-party sources say about you, since the model is summarising them rather than ranking your pages."
