sectionTwoHeading: "How we work"
sectionTwo: |
  ### We work where opinions actually form

  A buyer researching a purchase rarely stops at your website. They search your brand name alongside "review", "scam" or "alternative". They read a Reddit thread from two years ago. They skim a Quora answer written by a stranger. Those pages often outrank the brand's own, and they are what an answer engine retrieves when asked whether you are any good.

  ### We work within platform rules, deliberately

  Reddit, Quora and Wikipedia all prohibit undisclosed coordinated promotion, and India's consumer protection guidelines address paid reviews directly. Beyond the compliance risk, the practical problem is that these tactics fail loudly. A community that detects manipulation does not go quiet — it produces a new thread about being manipulated, which then ranks.

  So the work is slower and more literal than it sounds: genuine participation, expert answers that are actually expert, review requests to customers who actually bought something, and responses written like a person wrote them.

  ### We measure what answer engines say

  Rankings are no longer the whole picture. We track whether a brand is mentioned and cited in AI-generated answers across multiple models, because that is where a growing share of research now begins and ends.
