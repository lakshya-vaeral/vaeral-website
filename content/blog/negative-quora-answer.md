**You cannot delete someone else's Quora answer, and you should not expect Quora to delete it for you.** What you can do is change what the page says taken as a whole, which is what both a reader and a search engine actually see.

This is the same mechanic as [getting a negative Reddit post out of your search results](/blog/remove-reddit-post-google), with one significant difference: on Quora the answer order is contestable in a way a Reddit thread is not.

## Why the Quora page ranks for your brand at all

Two things stack. The question is usually phrased exactly as a worried buyer would search, often your company name plus "legit", "scam" or "worth it". And Quora is a strong domain, so a single thread can take a first-page position for a query your own site has no page for.

It gets worse in AI answers. In [Ahrefs' September 2026 analysis](https://ahrefs.com/blog/most-cited-domains-ai-overviews/), Quora is the sixth most-cited domain in Google AI Overviews. An unanswered complaint is not just a search result, it is a candidate source for what an AI tells someone about you.

## Can you get it removed?

Sometimes, and only on the grounds that actually exist.

Quora removes content that breaks its policies: harassment, personal information, plagiarism, impersonation. If the answer does one of those things, report it on that basis and say which policy it breaks.

If it is a real customer with a bad experience, it breaks no rule. Reporting it as spam will not work, and a failed report is not free: it is a record that you tried, and it is the kind of thing that gets screenshotted.

A factually false and damaging statement is a legal question rather than a moderation one. Take advice before sending anything that sounds like a threat, because a legal letter over an honest review is the single most reliable way to turn a small complaint into a large one.

## What actually works

**Answer the question yourself, with your affiliation stated.** Quora's rule is that [a user should disclose any relevant affiliations explicitly in an answer or in the credential that appears with the answer](https://help.quora.com/hc/en-us/articles/360055133711-What-is-Quora-s-policy-on-disclosing-affiliations-in-answers). Write as the company, say so, and answer the actual question rather than defending yourself.

This works better than it sounds. A specific, non-defensive reply that acknowledges the problem and says what changed reads as credible to the next person who lands there, and it is the version an AI engine is most likely to quote back.

**Get the question answered well by more than one person.** Answer order on Quora responds to engagement, not to seniority. A thread with one angry answer says one thing; a thread with one angry answer and three useful ones says something else, and the useful ones can end up above it.

**Give the query a better destination.** The underlying question, "is this company legitimate", deserves a page on your own site that answers it plainly. That is what [brand search result management](/services/brand-search-results) is for, and it addresses the root cause rather than the symptom.

## What does not work

**Vote manipulation and sockpuppet accounts.** They are against the rules, they are detectable, and enforcement takes the whole account with it, including every legitimate answer attached to it. The downside is not a warning, it is losing the asset you built.

**Arguing in the thread.** The second reply is where a complaint becomes a controversy. Answer once, well, and stop.

**Ignoring it.** Unlike a social post, these pages do not decay. A Quora question can rank for years and feed AI answers the entire time.

## How long it takes

Months. Answer order shifts over weeks as engagement accumulates, and search results lag behind that. There is no mechanism that makes it fast.
