**Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO)** is the practice of structuring content, schema markup and online entity data so AI-powered search engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Gemini can extract your content and cite it as a source in their answers.

It is not a replacement for SEO. On Google's own account, it is barely even a separate discipline.

## How is AEO different from SEO?

Less than the industry selling it would like. Google's published guidance is direct about this: ["From Google Search's perspective, optimizing for generative AI search is optimizing for the search experience, and thus still SEO."](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)

So the honest framing is not that AEO is a new discipline. It is that the emphasis has moved. Traditional SEO weights backlinks, domain authority and keyword placement. What now decides whether you are quoted in an answer leans harder on direct-answer structure, entity corroboration and whether anyone else on the internet says the same thing about you.

## Does a page still need to rank to get cited?

This is the part that genuinely changed, and it is measurable.

In 2025, being cited in an AI Overview mostly meant you already ranked. By 2026 that had stopped being true: [Ahrefs' March 2026 study of 863,000 search results pages and four million AI Overview URLs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-citations-top-10) found only 37.1% of cited URLs also ranked in the organic top ten for the same query, down from 76% in its previous analysis.

Ranking well still helps. It is no longer the gate it was, which is why a page can be pulled into an answer without winning the blue links, and why a page can own the blue links and never be quoted.

## Why does the structure of your content matter so much?

AI engines extract blocks of text based on signals such as HTML heading tags, paragraph position and schema. A page that states its key point in sentence one is easier to retrieve than a page that buries the answer in its third section.

This is answer-first or inverted-pyramid writing. It is concise for readers and technically legible for retrieval systems. ## What types of content get cited in AI answers?

The formats most useful for answer extraction are:

1. **Direct definition paragraphs**, "X is Y. It does Z."
2. **Numbered step lists**, where ordered steps have clear extraction boundaries.
3. **FAQ sections**, where question and answer pairs map directly to user prompts.
4. **Comparison content**, where "X versus Y" pages answer evaluation queries.
5. **Statistics with sources**, because cited figures are more trustworthy than unsupported claims.

Format is not the whole story. In [Ahrefs' September 2026 ranking of the most-cited domains in AI Overviews](https://ahrefs.com/blog/most-cited-domains-ai-overviews/), YouTube took 22.9% of citations and Quora was sixth at 4.7%. Platforms where real people answer questions are quoted heavily, which is a point about where the answer sits as much as how it is written. ## What does not work

Worth saying plainly, because a lot of AEO advice sells it.

**An `llms.txt` file is not a Google lever.** Google's guidance states that ["You don't need to create new machine readable files, AI text files, markup, or Markdown to appear in Google Search"](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), and that Google Search ignores them. They may still be read by other systems, so publishing one is harmless housekeeping. It is not a gap, and anyone pricing it as one is selling you a file.

**Rewriting copy to sound like an AI answer does not help either.** The signals that get content retrieved are structural and evidential, not stylistic.

## What is the relationship between AEO and entity building?

Entity building is the off-site component. AI engines synthesise information from many sources. When LinkedIn, directories, press coverage and the company website agree on the same description, an engine has stronger evidence for identifying and recommending the brand.

This is usually the real constraint. A site can be perfectly structured and still go uncited because nothing outside it corroborates what it says. ## How do you measure AEO performance?

There is no native dashboard. Measurement combines manual prompt testing, automated prompt tracking through available APIs, and citation monitoring to record whether a brand appears and which pages are referenced.

Expect the numbers to be small and noisy for a long time before they are not. Anyone promising a clean attribution line from AI answers to revenue is describing something that does not currently exist.
