**Quora works as a marketing channel when you treat it as a place to answer questions, and fails immediately when you treat it as a place to advertise.** That is not a moral point, it is a mechanical one: the platform collapses promotional answers, and a collapsed answer reaches nobody.

It is also worth more than it used to be. In [Ahrefs' September 2026 analysis](https://ahrefs.com/blog/most-cited-domains-ai-overviews/), Quora is the sixth most-cited domain in Google AI Overviews, so a good answer can now be quoted somewhere you never posted it.

## Step one: set the credential, once

Before writing anything, set the credential that appears beside your answers.

Quora's rule is that [a user should disclose any relevant affiliations explicitly in an answer or in the credential that appears with the answer](https://help.quora.com/hc/en-us/articles/360055133711-What-is-Quora-s-policy-on-disclosing-affiliations-in-answers). A credential like "Head of Support at [company]" satisfies this for every answer you write, which is simpler than remembering a disclosure line each time.

People respond better to a disclosed insider than to a vague expert. The person asking whether a product handles their edge case would rather hear from someone who builds it.

## Step two: find questions that already have an audience

The instinct is to search your keywords. Search the question instead, phrased the way a person would type it at eleven at night when something has gone wrong.

Filter what you find on three things:

1. **Does it already get views?** Quora shows view counts. A question with traffic and three weak answers is the opportunity. A question with no traffic is an empty room, however perfectly it matches your service.
2. **Can you answer it better than what is there?** If the top answer is genuinely good, pick another question.
3. **Would answering it be useful without mentioning your company?** If the honest answer never arrives anywhere near your product, you are not the right person to answer it.

## Step three: write the answer first, the marketing never

Open with the answer in the first two sentences. No throat-clearing, no "great question", no scene-setting. The reader is deciding in about four seconds whether to keep reading, and so is any engine deciding whether this block of text is worth extracting.

Then support it: the specifics, the exception, the thing people get wrong. Concrete detail is what makes an answer credible and what makes it quotable. The same structural logic applies as in [Answer Engine Optimisation](/blog/what-is-aeo), because an answer page and a search result are being read by the same kinds of systems.

Mention your company only where it is the honest answer to the question asked, and say that you work there. If the answer works without the mention, leave it out.

## Step four: do not lean on the link

Self-promotion that is not part of a direct and helpful response is not permitted, and answers that depend on an external site can be collapsed as promotional. Affiliate links are not allowed at all.

The practical test: delete the link and reread the answer. If it no longer says anything, the answer was an advertisement with a paragraph in front of it.

## What not to do

**Do not run multiple accounts.** Vote rings and sockpuppets are against the rules and are detectable. Enforcement removes the account and everything attached to it, so the downside is losing every answer that was working.

**Do not post the same answer on many questions.** It reads as spam to moderators and to readers, and it competes with itself.

**Do not ask questions in order to answer them yourself.** It is treated as manipulation, and it is usually obvious.

## What to expect

Slow and cumulative. A good answer gains views for years rather than spiking and dying, which makes this a poor channel for a launch and a good one for a category you intend to be in for a long time.

Measure it on answer views, profile views and referral sessions, and accept that the most valuable outcome, someone reading your answer inside an AI-generated response and never visiting Quora at all, is largely invisible.
