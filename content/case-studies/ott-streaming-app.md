problem: |
  The app existed. Barely anyone knew it did.

  In a category dominated by Netflix, Amazon Prime and Disney+, standing out requires more than a solid product. It requires a strong presence. The app had decent fundamentals but no visibility, no momentum, and no place in the conversations users were already having.

  In the streaming wars, unknown is the same as irrelevant.

  **Starting Point**
  - 3,480 reviews
  - 4.5★ Play Store rating
  - Negligible search visibility
  - Unmeasurable market share in a fiercely competitive category

  **Timeline:** 11 months, July 2025 to June 2026.
whatWeDid: |
  The objective was to own the conversations users were already having. The strategy revolved around increasing brand awareness through organic mentions.

  We focused on:
  - **Building narrative diversity:** reviews covered content variety, affordability, original programming, UI experience and family-friendly features, each capturing a different user intent
  - **Maintaining natural review velocity:** distribution was spread consistently over time to ensure authenticity and sustained momentum
  - **Owning comparison conversations:** when users searched "worth it vs Netflix?" or "best budget streaming app?", the app appeared backed by recent, credible user testimony
  - **Driving category presence** through volume, recency and content diversity across the review ecosystem

  Instead of competing on rating alone, we competed on presence.
results: |
  **The Turnaround**

  | Metric | July 2025 | June 2026 | Change |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Reviews | 3,480 | 91,100 | +2,517% |
  | Rating | 4.5★ | 4.4★ | Stabilised |
  | Market position | Invisible | Top-tier visibility | Dominant |

  **What Changed**
  - 87,620 new reviews over 11 months, around 7,965 a month
  - Play Store search rankings transformed, now placing top 5 for "streaming app", "OTT" and "affordable entertainment"
  - The app entered active comparison conversations as an equal alongside established competitors
  - Rating stabilised at 4.4★, a natural outcome of scale, and a credible signal at 91k reviews
  - Organic recommendations increased significantly as users began championing the app independently
  - Discovery accelerated through comparison searches, recommendation threads and category browsing

  **Why It Worked**

  In a category where every major player sits between 4.2★ and 4.7★, rating alone does not determine the winner. Presence does.

  The Play Store, Google Search and user behaviour all reward the same signals: volume builds legitimacy, recency drives relevance, diversity creates credibility, and momentum signals growth. A 4.4★ rating backed by 91,000 reviews carries more authority than a 4.8★ with 2,000.

  By ensuring the app appeared wherever users searched, compared and decided, it stopped being a footnote and became a fixture.

  **The Lesson**

  In a crowded category, scale is authority. More reviews mean more diverse and honest feedback, and people look for real opinions from real people.

  The app did not change. The world finally noticed it.
