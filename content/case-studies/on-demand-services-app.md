problem: |
  The app was performing well operationally, but its Play Store reputation was under pressure. Over a short period, the brand experienced a sudden surge in low-star reviews that appeared inconsistent with actual user sentiment and service performance. The volume and pattern suggested a coordinated attempt to damage the brand's reputation. The impact was immediate.

  **Starting Point**
  - 24,400 reviews
  - 3.6★ Play Store rating
  - Declining trust among prospective users
  - Growing competitive pressure

  For new users evaluating the app, the rating became a concern, even though the product itself remained strong fundamentally.
whatWeDid: |
  The objective was simple: ensure genuine customer voices became louder than the co-ordinated noise. We focused on:
  - Activating satisfied users to leave authentic reviews
  - Maintaining a consistent and natural review velocity
  - Encouraging detailed user feedback that reflected actual service experiences
  - Strengthening positive sentiment across the review ecosystem
  - Building rating stability over time rather than pursuing short-term spikes

  Instead of reacting to the attack, we concentrated on amplifying real customer experiences.
results: |
  **The Turnaround**

  | Metric | Month 1 | Month 3 | Change |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Reviews | 24.4k | 66.5k | +172% |
  | Rating | 3.6★ | 4.8★ | +1.2 Points |
  | Stability | Volatile | 4.8–4.9★ | Consistent |

  **What Changed**
  - 42,100 new positive reviews generated over 90 days
  - Positive feedback significantly outweighed negative sentiment
  - User confidence improved dramatically
  - The app regained credibility among prospective users
  - Store perception shifted from "high risk" to "high trust"
  - Rating remained stable at 4.8–4.9★ even after the campaign period

  **Why It Worked**
  In competitive categories, reputation often influences user decisions before the product ever gets a chance to. By increasing the visibility of authentic customer experiences, the app's public perception began to reflect the actual quality of the service. Fresh, genuine reviews helped restore trust, improve sentiment, and counteract the impact of negative review activity.

  **The Lesson**
  When a brand faces a review attack, the answer isn't panic. The answer is creating enough positive momentum that real customer experiences become impossible to ignore. This wasn't about changing the product. It was about ensuring the product's actual users had the strongest voice.

  In 90 days, the app moved from a vulnerable 3.6★ rating to a stable 4.8★, turning a reputation crisis into a growth story.
